Samantha Katcher Picard is a Principal Systems Engineer and data scientist specializing in cybersecurity, human‑centered systems design, and advanced analytics. With experience spanning government, research, and industry, she brings a multidisciplinary approach to solving complex technical challenges—integrating data science, cognitive security, and privacy engineering to strengthen mission systems and improve decision-making.
Samantha holds a Master of Science in Computer Science with a concentration in cybersecurity from Tufts University, where she was an MITRE Advanced Graduate Degree Program Fellow and a doctoral candidate (ABD). She also earned a Master of Science in Information Technology and a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology and Web Science with a dual major in Cognitive Science from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. She is currently pursuing a Doctor of Engineering in Engineering Technology at the University of Maine, focusing on Surveying Engineering Technology.
